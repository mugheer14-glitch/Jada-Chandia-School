School History

Govt. Primary School Jada Chandia (EMIS Code: 32330121) has been a cornerstone of quality primary education in the region. Established with the mission to impart foundational literacy and moral values to local children, the institution operates under the direct supervision of the Punjab Education Department. Over the years, the school has successfully nurtured thousands of students who have gone on to achieve remarkable heights.
